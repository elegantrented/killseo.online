Buying a used car used to mean a weekend at dealerships. Now you can shop, finance and arrange delivery from a phone. Selling has changed the same way: you can get an offer for your car without listing it or meeting strangers.

## Buying a used car online

- **Read the vehicle history report.** Look for accidents, title problems and how many owners it has had.
- **Understand the return policy.** Many online sellers offer a return window. Know exactly how long it is, the mileage limit and who pays for return shipping.
- **Get an independent inspection if you can.** A mechanic you choose can spot things photos cannot.
- **Compare the total price, not the monthly payment.** Fees, delivery charges and add-ons change the real cost.
- **Arrange your own financing quote first** so you can compare it with what the seller offers.

## Selling your car online

- **Get several offers.** Instant offers vary between buyers, sometimes by a lot.
- **Be accurate about condition.** Offers are usually adjusted after inspection if the car does not match the description.
- **Have your paperwork ready:** title, loan payoff information if you still owe money, and registration.
