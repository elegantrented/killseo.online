"Best business loan" depends entirely on what the money is for, how fast you need it, and what your business can qualify for. Comparing properly takes a little structure.

## The main types

- **Term loans:** a lump sum repaid over a fixed period. Good for one-off investments.
- **Lines of credit:** borrow, repay and borrow again up to a limit. Good for uneven cash flow.
- **SBA loans:** partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Often lower cost, with more paperwork and slower approval.
- **Equipment financing:** the equipment itself secures the loan.
- **Invoice financing:** borrow against unpaid customer invoices.

## What to compare

- **Total cost:** interest plus every fee, ideally expressed as an annual rate.
- **Repayment schedule:** daily, weekly or monthly, and whether it fits your cash flow.
- **Speed:** how fast funds arrive versus how much that speed costs.
- **Requirements:** time in business, revenue, credit, collateral, personal guarantee.
- **Prepayment:** whether paying early saves you anything.

## Before you apply

Have recent bank statements, tax returns, a profit-and-loss statement and a clear explanation of what the money is for. Lenders move faster when the paperwork is ready.
