New equipment can grow a business, whether it is a delivery van, kitchen equipment or a machine for the shop floor. Most businesses do not pay cash for it. **Equipment financing** spreads the cost over time.

## How equipment financing works

The lender pays for the equipment and you repay over a set term. Because the equipment itself secures the loan, qualifying can be easier than for an unsecured loan. If you stop paying, the lender can take the equipment back.

## Financing versus leasing

- **Financing:** you own the equipment once it is paid off.
- **Leasing:** lower payments, and you can upgrade at the end of the term, but you do not build ownership.

Equipment that loses value or goes out of date quickly is often a better fit for leasing.

## Working capital loans

Not every need is a piece of equipment. A **working capital loan** covers day-to-day costs such as payroll, inventory or a slow season. These are usually unsecured and shorter-term.

## What lenders look at

- Time in business and annual revenue
- Credit history (business and personal)
- Bank statements showing cash flow
- The equipment's cost and expected life

Some lenders work with **business loans for bad credit**, but expect higher costs. Compare carefully.
