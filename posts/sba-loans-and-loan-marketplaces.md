Applying to lenders one by one is slow. A **business loan marketplace** lets you submit one application and see options from multiple lenders. That is especially useful when you are weighing an **SBA loan** against faster alternatives.

## What SBA loans are

SBA loans are made by banks and other lenders and partly guaranteed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. The guarantee lowers the lender's risk, which often means longer terms and lower costs for the borrower.

The trade-off is paperwork and time. Expect to provide tax returns, financial statements, a business plan or use-of-funds explanation, and personal financial information.

## Common SBA programs

- **7(a) loans:** the general-purpose program, used for working capital, equipment, real estate and more.
- **504 loans:** for major fixed assets such as buildings or large equipment.
- **Microloans:** smaller amounts, often through nonprofit lenders.

## Why use a marketplace

- One application instead of many
- Side-by-side comparison of different loan types
- Access to lenders you may not have found yourself

Read each offer carefully. A marketplace shows options, but the loan terms come from the individual lender.
