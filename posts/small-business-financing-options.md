Banks turn down many small business loan applications, often because the business is young, credit is thin or the paperwork does not fit their box. That does not mean there are no options.

## Alternatives to a bank loan

- **Online term loans:** faster decisions and looser requirements, usually at a higher cost.
- **Business cash advance:** an advance repaid from future sales. Fast, but often expensive once you calculate the annual cost.
- **Working capital funding:** short-term money for operating costs.
- **Equipment financing:** easier to qualify for because the equipment secures it.
- **SBA microloans:** smaller amounts through mission-driven lenders.
- **Business credit cards:** useful for small, short-term needs if paid off quickly.

## Protect yourself

- Get the **total payback amount** in writing.
- Convert costs to an approximate annual rate so offers are comparable.
- Watch for daily payments that strain cash flow.
- Read the personal guarantee and default terms.
- Avoid stacking several advances at once.

## Improve your odds next time

Keep clean books, separate business and personal accounts, build business credit, and keep at least a few months of bank statements showing steady deposits.
