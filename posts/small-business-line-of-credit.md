Two of the most common products from online small business lenders are the **business line of credit** and the **short-term business loan**. Picking the right one saves money.

## Line of credit

You get a credit limit and draw from it when needed. You pay interest only on what you use, and as you repay, the credit becomes available again.

**Best for:** uneven cash flow, seasonal inventory, covering gaps between paying suppliers and getting paid by customers.

## Short-term loan

You receive a lump sum and repay it on a fixed schedule over a set term.

**Best for:** a specific one-time cost such as equipment, a renovation or a large order.

## Questions to ask any lender

- What is the total cost, including fees?
- How often are payments taken: daily, weekly or monthly?
- Is there a draw fee or maintenance fee on the line of credit?
- Is a personal guarantee or lien required?
- Does early repayment reduce the cost?

## Online versus bank lending

Online lenders typically decide faster and accept a wider range of businesses than traditional banks, but that convenience can come at a higher cost. If you have time and strong financials, get a bank quote too.
