## Questions to ask before you commit

- **What exactly am I getting?** Read the product description in full, not just the headline.
- **What does it cost in total?** Look for every fee, not only the advertised rate or price.
- **What are the requirements?** Check eligibility before you share personal or business details.
- **How long does it take?** Ask about timelines for approval, delivery or setup.
- **Who do I contact if something goes wrong?** Make sure there is a clear support channel.

## How to compare options

Get at least two or three quotes or offers for the same thing, and compare them on total cost, terms and flexibility, not just the first number you see. Read reviews from more than one source, and be cautious of any offer that pressures you to decide immediately.
