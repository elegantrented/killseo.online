## Step 1: Know what you need

Before you look at any provider, write down what you actually need, your budget and your timeline. It is much easier to judge an offer when you know your own numbers.

## Step 2: Read the full terms

Advertised headlines rarely tell the whole story. Look for:

- the total cost, including all fees
- any long-term commitments or penalties for ending early
- what happens if your situation changes
