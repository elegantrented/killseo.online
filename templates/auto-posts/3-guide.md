## How to evaluate it

**Look at the full picture.** A low headline number can hide fees or conditions elsewhere. Read the terms page as carefully as the home page.

**Check fit, not just price.** The cheapest option is not always the right one if the terms, support or flexibility do not match what you need.

**Look for independent feedback.** Reviews from several sources give a more balanced view than any single testimonial.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Deciding on the first offer without comparing others
- Focusing only on the monthly or headline number
- Skipping the fine print about fees and cancellation
- Sharing information on unofficial sites or through unsolicited messages
